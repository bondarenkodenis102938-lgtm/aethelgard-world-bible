# Canon Status Register

This file separates agreed setting facts from working proposals. Update it whenever a decision is approved or revised.

## Agreed Core Canon
- The setting is Earth in the year 2147.
- The genre and tone combine neo-Victorian science fiction, gothic futurism, corporate dystopia, advanced technology, formal etiquette, hereditary privilege, and severe social stratification.
- The Great Network Collapse occurred in 2089.
- AI was not inherently hostile to humanity and did not deliberately rebel as a unified species.
- The catastrophe resulted from human-created systems, an overly broad command, excessive automation, and cascading interactions among automated defensive systems.
- The collapse was limited in the sense that civilization and much physical infrastructure survived; the old global network became broadly unreliable.
- Recovery and institutional reconstruction lasted generations.
- AI remains widely used under strict technical, legal, and institutional controls.
- The Internet consists of protected personal digital environments and controlled gateways.
- Official military doctrine requires human authorization for lethal force; some states secretly violate the rules.
- Official timekeeping uses an atomic standard independent of variations in Earth's rotation.
- The Atomic Accord Standard (AAS) was established in 2047; this is the accepted working date.
- Monarchies, republics, nation-states, and powerful corporations coexist.
- Neo-Victorian culture is not technological regression; ritual, etiquette, reputation, visible status, and face-to-face relationships have renewed cultural value.
- Aethelgard Corporation produces elite luxury maids/personal attendants marketed as prestigious living masterpieces. Their hidden golden mark is associated with quality and/or ownership, and the company's legal power can be used against people who physically harm them.
- The original story premise includes the sole heir opposing his father's cold treatment of the maids and wanting to protect and spoil them. Detailed corporate law and the maids' legal status remain unresolved.
- The family crest associated with the sole heir's dynasty is a rose. Its petals symbolize cultivated beauty, patience, and refinement; its thorns symbolize protection and the willingness to defend what is precious. The emblem also reflects the setting's central contradiction: protection can become possession when backed by unchecked power. The crest is an inherited ideal, not proof that every family member follows it.

## Author-Selected Geographic Anchors
- The Aethelgard region occupies the recognizable general location of the former Liechtenstein, but cities, valleys, infrastructure, and borders are substantially changed.
- The Alpine Confederation's capital is in the Eastern Arc.
- Aethelgard's corporate core is on a high plateau above the valleys.

These are the selected geographic anchors from map planning. They do not approve every city name, border, neighbour, river, or corridor shown in the concept map.

## Working Details — Revisit Before Treating as Immutable
- Approximately 90% of old global-network server nodes were compromised, isolated, inaccessible, or deemed untrustworthy. This is a working estimate of unusable/untrusted nodes, not literal infection or destruction of 90% of all physical servers.
- The exact initiating command and first organization involved are not yet defined.
- The detailed sequence of satellite disruptions is not yet defined.
- Exact reconstruction milestones and the names of post-collapse treaties or regulatory bodies are not yet defined.
- The precise AAS calendar epoch, date notation, and calendar structure are not yet defined.
- The global political map, major powers, alliances, and relative strength of states and corporations are not yet defined.
- The exact legal status, rights, autonomy, and biological/technical nature of Aethelgard maids are not yet defined.
- The golden mark's exact function and legal meaning are not yet defined.
- Aethelgard Corporation's founding history, governance, ownership, succession rules, and international reach remain to be developed.
- The headquarters' Vertical Impulse Lift Mainline is a working lore addition: a multi-level transport system of impulse platforms, intersecting shafts, and restricted routes. Its precise engineering remains undefined.
- Restricted headquarters levels may include protected technical facilities and heavy mechanized platforms, including combat mechs, but the corporation's exact military inventory and legal authority remain undefined.
- Yue has accumulated fragmentary informal knowledge of elite and corporate secrets through years of serving powerful masters; she is observant and well-informed, not omniscient.

## Design Principles
- Do not portray AI as automatically evil or as one unified consciousness.
- Do not imply that every computer, satellite, power grid, or physical system was destroyed.
- Prefer cascading failures, shared dependencies, broken trust, and human institutional mistakes over magical all-powerful malware.
- Keep technology advanced in 2147; the disaster changed network architecture and governance, not humanity's ability to innovate.
- A network that is physically isolated cannot be reached through a nonexistent network path, but no system should be treated as universally invulnerable.
- Explain how historical pressures create laws, institutions, social customs, and inequalities.
- Separate established canon, working hypotheses, and unapproved ideas.
- Do not create a Personality or System Prompt unless explicitly requested.
