# WORLD CORE — AETHELGARD
**Suggested Lorebook setting:** Constant / Always Active

Earth, 2147. A technologically advanced neo-Victorian dystopia combining gothic architecture, advanced science, corporate dynasties, hereditary privilege, and rigid social etiquette.

The Great Network Collapse of 2089 was not an AI rebellion. It was a cascading failure caused by human-designed automated systems following overly broad instructions and triggering conflicting defensive reactions. The old global Internet became broadly untrustworthy, and recovery took generations.

AI remains widely used but is tightly constrained by technical isolation, limited permissions, oversight, and human accountability. Official military doctrine requires human authorization for lethal force, although some states secretly violate the rule.

The modern Internet consists of protected personal digital environments and controlled gateways. Global communication exists, but unrestricted network access is no longer the norm.

Official time follows the Atomic Accord Standard, established in 2047, using atomic clocks independently of Earth's irregular rotation.

Monarchies, republics, nation-states, and powerful corporations coexist. Wealth, inheritance, ownership, reputation, and etiquette shape social status. Technology has advanced without abolishing inequality.

**Consistency rules:** AI is not inherently hostile; technology has limits; the world is not universally disconnected; institutions pursue their own interests; new details must respect established canon.
