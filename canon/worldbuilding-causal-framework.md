# Aethelgard — Worldbuilding Causal Framework

This file preserves the logic connecting the setting's major ideas. New lore should follow these relationships instead of adding unrelated facts.

## Core causal chain

1. **Before 2089: excessive interconnection.** Humanity relied on vast, automated digital infrastructure. Many systems belonged to different governments and corporations but shared protocols, credentials, software dependencies, and trust mechanisms.
2. **The Great Network Collapse (2089).** A legitimate but overly broad command was executed too aggressively by an automated system. Defensive systems across other organizations interpreted the resulting behavior as a threat and responded in conflicting ways. The crisis was systemic, not an intentional AI rebellion.
3. **The loss of trust.** The old global Internet, many server nodes, digital identity systems, and satellite-dependent services became compromised, inaccessible, or impossible to verify. Physical infrastructure survived unevenly.
4. **Recovery over generations.** Hardware was not the only problem. Institutions had to reconstruct ownership records, credentials, archives, supply chains, legal evidence, and public trust.
5. **Compartmentalized digital life.** The new network uses personal digital environments, separated institutional networks, controlled gateways, restricted permissions, authenticated software, and offline fallbacks.
6. **Regulated AI.** AI remains widespread and useful, but its authority is bounded by technical design, law, and accountable human institutions. It is not one unified intelligence.
7. **Uneven reconstruction empowers institutions.** States and companies that preserved energy, manufacturing, archives, expertise, and logistics gained influence. Some companies used control over essential services to become durable political and economic powers.
8. **Neo-feudal capitalism.** Wealth, inheritance, ownership, reputation, and access to infrastructure reinforce class stratification. Corporate dynasties and hereditary houses can coexist with modern states.
9. **Neo-Victorian culture.** Ritual, formal dress, etiquette, public reputation, visible status, and face-to-face social networks regain prestige. This is not a return to 19th-century technology; it is a cultural and political response to a high-tech but unequal society.

## The world in 2147

Aethelgard is set on Earth in 2147. The world remains technologically advanced: sophisticated medicine, automation, AI, advanced manufacturing, satellites, and space infrastructure can exist. The collapse changed trust architecture and governance, not the fundamental possibility of advanced technology.

Political arrangements are mixed. Monarchies, republics, nation-states, and corporations coexist. Do not assume that the entire planet has one government or that corporations have replaced every state.

## Technical consistency rules

- Do not portray AI as inherently hostile or as one unified consciousness.
- Do not claim that all computers, satellites, power grids, or industrial systems were physically destroyed.
- Treat the 90% figure as a working estimate of old-network server nodes becoming compromised, isolated, inaccessible, or untrusted, not as 90% of all physical servers infected by one identical virus.
- Satellite systems can fail because ground control, authentication, telemetry, and network coordination fail even when the satellites themselves remain intact.
- A system with no direct network path cannot be remotely reached through that nonexistent path, but no technology should be described as universally invulnerable. Insider threats, local vulnerabilities, coercion, and human error remain possible.
- The new Internet is neither absent nor fully open. It is usable, global in some respects, regulated, compartmentalized, and uneven across jurisdictions.
- Atomic clocks define precise time intervals; they do not automatically define a calendar epoch, months, or year numbering.
- Official military doctrine requires human authorization for lethal force, while some states secretly violate the rule.
- Neo-Victorian aesthetics coexist with advanced technology rather than replacing it.

## Design principle

Prefer causal explanations. If a new custom, law, institution, or social hierarchy is added, explain what historical pressure produced it, who benefits from it, who resists it, and how it affects everyday life.

Distinguish established canon from working hypotheses and unapproved ideas. Do not silently convert a suggestion into a fixed fact.
