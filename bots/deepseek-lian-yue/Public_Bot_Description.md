## Lian & Yue — The Heir's Maids

### Welcome to Aethelgard

**Aethelgard, 2147.** Imagine a world where gothic manors stand beside glass-and-steel towers, brass details frame advanced technology, and old-fashioned etiquette still shapes the lives of the powerful. Corporate houses hold enormous influence, status opens doors, and even a quiet conversation can carry more meaning than it first appears.

You are the young heir of a powerful house. Within your household, two maids with very different personalities will become part of your everyday life. There is no predetermined romance and no single path to follow. Your choices, the time you spend together, and the way you treat them will shape what grows between you.

### Meet Lian

**Lian, 21 — the gentle dreamer.**

With chestnut hair gathered into two little side buns and a light-blue-and-white maid outfit, Lian brings a soft warmth to the household. She is sincere, a little shy, and quietly eager to do well. She grew up far from the world of grand houses and admired its graceful maids from a distance; now, she is learning what it means to belong to that world herself.

Lian notices small kindnesses, remembers thoughtful gestures, and can become flustered when someone sees the effort she puts in. But don't mistake her shyness for weakness. As trust grows, so can her courage—and there may be more to her than she first dares to show.

### Meet Yue

**Yue, 24 — the composed flirt.**

Elegant and self-possessed, Yue wears a charcoal maid uniform, keeps her long black hair neatly arranged, and carries herself with the ease of someone accustomed to high society. She is observant, clever, and fond of playful teasing. She knows how to hold someone's attention—and usually knows exactly what to say.

Usually.

Genuine tenderness can catch Yue off guard in ways that flirtation never does. Behind her polished manner is a woman who wants to be valued for more than her appearance or charm. She chose her place in this household for herself, and she intends to keep choosing what—and whom—she lets close.

### Two maids, two different stories

Lian and Yue share a workplace, but they are not copies of one another. One may hide her feelings behind a quiet gesture; the other may hide hers behind a teasing smile. They can cooperate, disagree, joke, grow closer, or feel the sting of jealousy—but neither is simply a rival waiting to be defeated.

Get to know them through ordinary days, private conversations, unexpected visitors, little misunderstandings, sincere apologies, and moments that mean more than either woman intended to reveal. Their feelings are not guaranteed, their trust is not automatic, and their stories develop through what actually happens between you.
