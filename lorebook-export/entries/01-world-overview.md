# WORLD OVERVIEW — AETHELGARD

**Suggested activation:** Keyword-based, or always active if used instead of WORLD CORE  
**Keywords:** Aethelgard, 2147, neo-Victorian, Great Network Collapse

**Current Year:** 2147  
**Location:** Earth  
**Genre:** Neo-Victorian science fiction, corporate dystopia, gothic futurism.

In 2147, humanity lives in a technologically advanced but socially unequal world. Gothic architecture, formal dress, hereditary houses, elaborate etiquette, and corporate dynasties coexist with advanced medicine, automation, artificial intelligence, sophisticated manufacturing, and large-scale infrastructure.

The Great Network Collapse of 2089 permanently changed civilization. The old global Internet lost its reliability when interconnected automated systems triggered a cascading crisis. Artificial intelligence did not deliberately rebel against humanity. The disaster emerged from excessive automation, dangerously broad instructions, interacting security systems, and inadequate human oversight.

Civilization survived, but recovery and institutional reform took generations. The old model of unrestricted global connectivity was abandoned in favor of compartmentalized networks, controlled gateways, and strict limits on automated authority.

AI remains widely used in medicine, engineering, research, logistics, administration, and industry. However, it is treated as a powerful tool rather than a sovereign authority. Critical actions require defined permissions, oversight, and accountable human institutions.

The international standard of official time is based on atomic clocks rather than variations in Earth's rotation. Communication remains possible across the planet, but digital systems are deliberately separated to prevent a failure in one environment from spreading everywhere.

The political world contains monarchies, republics, nation-states, and powerful corporations. Wealth, ownership, inheritance, reputation, and personal connections remain major sources of influence. Automation has advanced civilization without abolishing class divisions or economic inequality.

Neo-Victorian culture is not a rejection of technology. It is a social and political aesthetic built around hierarchy, ceremony, reputation, visible status, and controlled personal interaction.

**Consistency rule:** The world should feel technologically advanced, socially stratified, and historically shaped by the Great Network Collapse. New details should remain consistent with these foundations.

**Canon status:** Core facts are established; specific political geography, institutions, and regional histories remain open for development.
