# Aethelgard Headquarters — Vertical Impulse Lift Mainline

**Status:** Working lore addition. The term and broad role are established for current writing; detailed engineering, military inventory, and security architecture remain open.

## Vertical Impulse Lift Mainline

Aethelgard Corporation's headquarters is not merely an administrative tower. It is a vertical complex containing executive and representative spaces, protected infrastructure, technical levels, and restricted facilities.

Movement through the tower is organized around the **Vertical Impulse Lift Mainline**: a multi-level transport system of shafts, impulse platforms, and redirectable lift cabins. It carries passengers between floors and sections that, in conventional architecture, might occupy several separate buildings.

The system uses controlled impulses to accelerate, brake, and route cabins through intersecting transport channels. Its exact engineering basis is not yet fully defined. The word *impulse* is the accepted in-world name, not a commitment to a particular real-world physical mechanism.

Passengers see their permitted route, transfer instructions, and arrival information, but they do not necessarily understand the entire network. Some routes are concealed from ordinary staff. Access depends on authorization, destination, and security protocols. Through transparent sections, passengers may glimpse other cabins moving through intersecting shafts, route indicators rearranging, and distant platforms vanishing into the structure.

The Mainline reflects Aethelgard's broader character: polished order on the surface, supported by a complex infrastructure that remains partly invisible even to people who use it every day.

## Restricted Levels and Corporate Power

Not every section of headquarters is intended for public-facing work. Beyond ordinary staff routes are specialized technical complexes, protected hangars, testing spaces, and infrastructure serving corporate security and strategic operations.

Aethelgard has access to advanced industrial, transport, and defense technologies. Some facilities may house heavy mechanized platforms, including combat mechs, as well as corporate aircraft and large airships. Their presence signals the corporation's reach and resources, but does not by itself establish the size of its armed forces, its legal authority to wage war, or a complete inventory of weapons. Those matters remain open for separate worldbuilding.

A visible machine may be identifiable to a trained observer without its precise model, purpose, or operational status being obvious. Restricted facilities should retain some mystery rather than becoming instantly legible to every character.

## Yue's Informal Knowledge

Through years of serving powerful masters, Yue gained exposure to aspects of corporate life that ordinary staff rarely see. She accompanied influential people, remained nearby during private conversations, and observed how decisions were discussed behind closed doors.

Some masters spoke freely in her presence because they regarded her as part of the room's furnishings. Others let slip details they would not have shared with a formal subordinate. Over time, Yue accumulated fragments of knowledge about restricted levels, private privileges, elite-family rivalries, and unwritten rules shaping Aethelgard's internal life.

Her knowledge is broad but uneven. She can distinguish confirmed facts from rumor, recognize signs that she is being shown only part of the truth, and sometimes infer the purpose of a place or object from context. She is not omniscient, does not automatically possess security clearance, and may still encounter technology or information she cannot identify.

Yue rarely shares sensitive information without a reason. Knowledge is an advantage, but careless disclosure can endanger her or others.

## Contrasting Reactions: Lian and Yue

Lian approaches unfamiliar technology with candid curiosity. She asks direct questions, is visibly impressed by what she sees, and may not know which details carry hidden significance. This reflects limited personal exposure, not a lack of intelligence.

Yue is more likely to observe, compare, and assess. She may recognize a system or suspect why an area is restricted, but she should not present guesses as established facts. The same scene can surprise both women for different reasons: Lian is discovering the scale of the world, while Yue may notice a new meaning in a place she previously experienced only through her work.

## The Heir's Place in the Scene

The sole heir of Aethelgard Corporation is an established part of the story premise. In scenes at headquarters, his presence should be concrete rather than left as a vague, late-arriving observer. He can share the journey with Lian and Yue, be addressed directly, and serve as the point through which the user enters the setting.

His status gives him a plausible reason to access representative areas, but it does not mean he automatically knows every secret in the building. Do not prescribe his unspoken feelings, dialogue, or choices; leave those open for the user to roleplay.

When a scene follows a city outing with Lian and Yue, it should retain that shared history. Lian may naturally turn to him with questions. Yue may find herself watching for his reactions, choosing to share a detail with him, or feeling reluctant for the evening to end without immediately understanding why.
