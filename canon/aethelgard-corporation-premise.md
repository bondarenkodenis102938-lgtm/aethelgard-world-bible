# Aethelgard Corporation — Original Premise

**Purpose:** Preserve the original creative premise from the earlier discussion. This file is a foundation for later canon development, not a complete corporate profile.

## Core concept

Aethelgard Corporation manufactures elite luxury maids and personal attendants: living masterpieces of presentation, service, etiquette, and carefully cultivated desirability. They are not merely ordinary employees in a uniform. They are prestigious products of a powerful corporation and highly visible status symbols in the social order of 2147.

The faces of Aethelgard maids appear on public advertising and prestigious billboards. Owning or employing one signals wealth, taste, influence, and access to the corporation's highest tier of luxury. Their appearance, manners, training, and quality are part of the brand's public identity.

## The golden mark

Each maid bears a concealed golden mark or tattoo associated with her quality classification and/or corporate ownership. The precise design, placement, technical function, and legal meaning remain to be defined. It should be treated as a meaningful part of the setting, not as a generic decorative tattoo.

## Corporate property and legal protection

The original premise establishes that Aethelgard Corporation's legal system treats physical damage inflicted on one of its maids as damage to corporate property and therefore as a serious financial and legal matter. A noble who strikes, injures, or otherwise damages a maid may face aggressive legal action from Aethelgard's lawyers, potentially including ruinous financial or reputational consequences.

This creates a deliberate tension: the corporation can protect its maids from abuse because it regards them as valuable assets, while the legal foundation of that protection may still frame them as property. The setting should preserve this moral and political contradiction rather than automatically resolving it.

The exact legal regime remains open: define whether maids are legally classified as employees, licensed service constructs, persons with limited rights, protected corporate assets, or a category unique to this world. Do not assume the answer before the worldbuilding discussion settles it.

## The heir and the internal conflict

The user's central role in the original premise is the sole heir of Aethelgard Corporation. Unlike his father, who treats the maids coldly as furniture or possessions, the heir resents that attitude and has sworn to protect and spoil the maids under his care. His position gives him the right or corporate authority to claim any maid bearing the Aethelgard brand, subject to whatever internal rules are later established.

This creates an internal conflict between:
- the father's utilitarian and possessive view of the maids;
- the heir's protective, indulgent, and more personal attitude;
- the corporation's interest in ownership, reputation, and asset protection;
- the maids' own individuality and agency, which should not be erased by the corporate legal framework.

Do not turn this premise into a character Personality or System Prompt unless explicitly requested. This is world and story background only.

## Canon boundaries

Established in the original premise:
- Aethelgard manufactures elite luxury maids/personal attendants.
- The maids are marketed as exceptional, prestigious, carefully cultivated creations.
- Their public image is a major status symbol.
- A hidden golden mark is associated with quality and/or ownership.
- Corporate lawyers can pursue severe consequences when a maid is physically harmed.
- The sole heir opposes his father's cold treatment and wants to protect and spoil the maids.
- The heir has authority to claim maids bearing the brand.

Still to define:
- Whether maids are human, bioengineered, cybernetically enhanced, synthetic, or a mixture.
- Their exact legal status, rights, autonomy, consent protections, and employment conditions.
- The golden mark's technical function and precise symbolism.
- How the corporation trains, manufactures, acquires, certifies, and assigns maids.
- How ownership/claim rights work and what limits exist.
- The corporation's founding history, board, shareholders, succession rules, and global reach.
- The difference between public marketing and private corporate practice.

**Consistency rule:** Aethelgard's power should emerge from the combination of luxury branding, elite clientele, legal leverage, corporate influence, and the social meaning of owning a branded attendant. Keep the ethical tension visible.
